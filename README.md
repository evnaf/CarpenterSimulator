# Carpenter Simulator

**A fun, immersive woodcraft and furniture-building simulation game**

---

## 🚧 Development Roadmap (2025)

### **Q2 2025: Initial Launch and Early Features**
- **Alpha Version 1.0**: Release the first playable version with basic tree chopping mechanics.
- **Woodworking Basics**: Add the ability to chop down trees, strip bark, and cut wood into manageable pieces.
- **First Contracts**: Introduce the first batch of simple furniture contracts to start the player's journey.

### **Q3 2025: Expanding the Crafting Experience**
- **Beta Version 1.5**: Expand gameplay with more detailed wood processing (e.g., treating wood, shaping wood).
- **Enhanced Workshop Tools**: Add new tools like saws, drills, and hammers for crafting furniture.
- **Advanced Furniture**: Introduce a variety of furniture designs with more intricate requirements.
- **Customer Orders**: Begin integrating customer orders based on random generation, with different difficulty levels.

### **Q4 2025: Polishing and Adding Depth**
- **Version 2.0**: Complete the first major update with a fully functional crafting system.
- **Expanded Workshop**: Add multiple workshop areas with different themes and upgrades.
- **New Mechanics**: Introduce seasonal events, limited-time orders, and new materials.
- **Market System**: Introduce a marketplace where players can sell completed furniture.

### **Q1 2026: Post-Launch Support and Expansion**
- **Version 2.5**: Focus on bug fixes, quality of life improvements, and performance optimizations.
- **Modding Support**: Begin adding modding tools for the community to create custom furniture, tools, and orders.
- **Community Engagement**: Host events, challenges, and feature community-created content.

## ⚙️ Core Mechanics

### 1. **Sourcing and Preparing Wood**
- **Chopping Trees**: Players will start by sourcing wood from different types of trees scattered around the environment. Each tree type offers different qualities of wood.
- **Stripping Bark**: After chopping down a tree, players can strip bark to gather different materials and textures.
- **Wood Cutting**: The wood will need to be cut into usable planks or pieces, which players will later use in crafting furniture.

### 2. **Get Contracts for Furniture**
- **Customer Orders**: Randomized contracts will appear, specifying the type of furniture the customer wants, along with the materials and time constraints.
- **Difficulty Levels**: Orders will range in difficulty, with simple items like chairs and tables, to more advanced items like bookshelves or cabinets.
- **Time Management**: Some orders will have time-sensitive requirements, adding pressure to complete tasks efficiently.

### 3. **Use Your Workshop and Tools to Create Furniture**
- **Tools and Workbenches**: Players will use a variety of tools in their workshop, such as saws, drills, hammers, and chisels, to shape the wood into furniture.
- **Crafting Stations**: Different workbenches and crafting stations will be used for specific tasks (e.g., a carving station for intricate designs, or a sanding station for finishing).
- **Assembly**: Players will need to assemble the furniture using the correct pieces of wood, nails, screws, and other materials they’ve gathered.
- **Customization**: Players will be able to customize furniture with finishes, stains, and paints, which could affect the quality and price of the finished product.
